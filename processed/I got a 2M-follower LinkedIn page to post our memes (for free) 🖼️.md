## Meme pages are desperate for content 😩

They have to post something funny every week, and being funny is HARD.

So when you send them a free meme, they’ll often post it and tag you. Their followers see your name → some then follow you.

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!X122!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F00a605e3-fff5-471b-ae23-37220cfea10a_594x549.png)

## Where most marketers go wrong ❌

Let me give you a test:

You want a meme page with a million followers to post about your product. What would you send them?

Most marketers would send one of these:

- `“Can you share our product launch in a funny way?”`
- `“How much for a sponsored post?”`

**Both are wrong. ❌**

Big meme pages make money from sponsored posts, and a launch post is exactly that. So they ignore the free version (your first email) and send their rates for the paid one (your second).

**Just send them the meme itself. ✅**

I mean a finished post about a problem their followers face every day. All they have to do is take it and hit “post”.

## My playbook 💻

Here’s how you can do it:

### 1\. Create a joke about the problem you solve 🪄

Steal this meme template:

**“When \[your customer\] FINALLY \[gets what your product gives them\]”**

Our meme was a CISO (head of security) who *FINALLY* reached 0 critical issues. Every security team dreams about that day, and it’s what Wiz sells.

For an invoicing tool, it can be like “When the client *FINALLY* pays on time”.

On the day King Charles was crowned, the same LinkedIn page posted this meme from Entitle (another security startup):

Same line we stole! 😂

For trendy templates, there’s [r/MemeTemplatesOfficial](https://www.reddit.com/r/MemeTemplatesOfficial/) on Reddit.

### 2\. Target big LinkedIn pages that don’t call themselves meme pages 🤫

Start with the pages your customers already follow:

Open the LinkedIn profiles of a few of your customers, scroll down to “Interests” and click “Companies”. If they follow a meme page, it shows up there. Then search LinkedIn for `“[your niche] humor”`, `“[your niche] hub”` or `“daily [your niche]”` and filter by Companies.

Now listen:

**Pages that call themselves meme pages rarely get traction.** In my experience, the really good ones call themselves the hub for their audience, or its daily read, even when all they post is memes:

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!JBLx!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7cb74b45-e2cc-483e-a334-093be0775398_1900x522.png)

After you find a good page, find more like it. Check the “Similar pages” box on the right.

### 3\. How to find their email address 💌

No hack here. It’s probably public.

Their email is usually in the page’s “About” section or its Linktree. I found The Cyber Security Hub’s online, just by searching for it. Nothing special.

Before you email one, check two things:

- It posted something this week.
- It already credits other brands (“via \[company\]” in the caption). The Cyber Security Hub [still does](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/the-cyber-security-hub_via-pentera-activity-7400881290226589696-esKM).

### 4\. Send them this exact email ✏️

This is my first email to them, the one with the too-nerdy GIF. I even introduced Wiz as “the largest cybersecurity unicorn in the world”:

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!u9rm!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Ff69ba882-6b8b-4cea-b50c-4dfc31d8c7fe_1042x780.png)

Make sure you offer them full rights and an optional tag.

Copy my template:

> **Subject:** Would you like to use this meme?
> 
> Hey!
> 
> I’m \[name\] from \[company\] (we make \[product\] for \[the people who follow your page\]). We made this funny meme:
> 
> \[the meme\]
> 
> **Do you want to publish it?** You have full rights, and feel free to tag us on LinkedIn (@\[company\]) if you’d like! 😊
> 
> Best,  
> \[name\]

You can even write the caption for them too. They used mine almost word for word on our periodic table. And when you’re ready with the next meme, reply in the same thread and brag about the last one’s results.

Find the biggest pages in your niche and send them a meme about the problem you solve. Worst case, they ignore you and you post it on your own page.
