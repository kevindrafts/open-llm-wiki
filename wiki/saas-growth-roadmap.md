Corey Haines's Conversion Factory roadmap sequences SaaS growth work into foundation, base, and expansion. It starts with customer research and positioning, carries that work through conversion and activation, then expands acquisition and testing once buyers have a clear path from first visit to product value. Haines frames it as an agency approach for early SaaS companies working toward $1M ARR or past a plateau.

# SaaS growth roadmap

## Foundation

Audit positioning, branding, site copy, onboarding, email, and pricing. Prioritize the gaps, then interview customers about what triggered the switch, which alternatives they considered, and what they hoped would improve. Combine switch interviews with surveys and competitor research. Turn the findings into a shared positioning document and use it to update the brand and homepage. See [[website-conversion-and-b2b-lead-generation]] for the homepage sequence.

## Base

Build the pages that support buying decisions, including use cases, features, competitor comparisons, pricing, signup, and demo requests. Give sales the case studies and ROI tools needed to support those decisions. Improve first-run activation and use behavior-triggered email for trial conversion and re-engagement. Test pricing models and the way each offer is explained.

## Expansion

Test messaging and CTAs against customer conversion where volume permits. Use clicks and form submissions to diagnose upstream friction. Expand through launches, paid campaigns with dedicated landing pages, and high-intent search content. Consider localized pages and payments when entering new markets.

The source outlines agency deliverables. It supplies no phase gates or measured results for the full sequence. As an application of the roadmap, prioritize the current bottleneck and use paid ad clicks as early evidence about messaging, then check whether that message attracts customers who activate and pay.
