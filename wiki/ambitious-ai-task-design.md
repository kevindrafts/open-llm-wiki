# Ambitious AI task design

## Four dimensions

Rieseberg distinguishes speed, altitude, depth, and range:

- Speed reduces time spent on existing work.
- Altitude asks for the underlying outcome. A quarterly presentation can become a system for detecting problems and informing resource decisions.
- Depth uses expert methods, raw data, assumption checks, and independent evaluation.
- Range borrows techniques from other disciplines. Expert roleplay alone adds little.

## Applying the framework

His crash-prevention example requires accessible crash reports, mappings between releases and commits, and machines for reproducing failures. Broader responsibility depends on that infrastructure.

He recommends testing predictions on unseen data and using external checks. Treat the article as practitioner guidance, not a measured comparison of prompting strategies.
