## How It Works

You build an AI that knows a business's website, then add it to their site as a chat box and a voice agent. Customers get answers fast, then book or leave their number. Bob builds it all in GoHighLevel.

**How Bob found it:** Customers at his phone repair shop kept asking the same questions about price, speed and whether they'd lose their data. He put an AI on the shop's website and was shocked by the results.

## The Numbers

**His phone repair shop, per month:**

- 350 to 500 website visits
- About 130 conversations with the AI
- About 60 booked appointments or callback requests

**Your costs and time:**

- Software at $300 to $400 a month, plus some AI usage
- About 1 to 2 hours a month on a $300 client
- About 10 to 20 hours a month on a $4,000 client

**Bob's rule of thumb is that about 5% of a site's visitors talk to the AI and about 2.5% turn into leads. Pair that with a client's traffic and lead value, and you know what the AI is worth to them.**

### Step 1: Pick Businesses Where a Lead Is Worth a Lot

Bob said this works best when customers need something now, like HVAC, plumbing, phone repair or auto. He skips businesses with low traffic or a full voicemail box.

Then stay in one industry. Bob's is forklifts and pallet racking, where he said referrals come easy.

Bob uses GoHighLevel's AI Studio to copy one page of the prospect's own site, adds the chat and voice widgets, and publishes it.

He prints the page with the URL highlighted in yellow and brings his laptop. It takes him 30 minutes to an hour.

### Step 3: Ask ChatGPT for Five Demo Questions

Before the meeting, Bob types this into ChatGPT or Perplexity: "I've just created a voice AI and a chat widget for this website. What five questions would you have me ask the voice AI in front of the owner to prove how good it is?"

For a security company, one question was about their cannabis industry work, a specialty on their site. The owners went nuts.

On cold calls, Bob doesn't lead with AI, because people either love it or hate it. He pitches 10% to 20% more leads off their website and runs it free for about four weeks, with no credit card.

### Step 5: Price It Off What a Lead Is Worth

Bob asks AI what a lead is worth in that industry, then gets the owner's real traffic from Google Analytics. If the trial brought in $30,000 worth of leads, $3,000 a month makes sense. If it brought in $400, it's more like $100.

### Step 6: Move the Chat Box Up and Keep Tuning

Bob said people learned to ignore the chat box in the bottom corner. He puts the AI near the top and changed "Do you need help?" to "Need a quick answer?" After launch, you fix its mistakes and improve the knowledge base, and Bob said that's what clients really pay for.

## Mistakes to Avoid

- **Showing someone else's demo.** Bob's forklift prospects couldn't picture his phone repair AI on their site.
- **Guessing on value.** Bob admitted his early pitch was basically "it should work." Run their numbers first.
- **Setting it and forgetting it.** Bob said when it starts to feel easy, you're probably missing things.
- **Hiding that it's AI.** Even with a cloned voice, have it say it's an AI guide.
